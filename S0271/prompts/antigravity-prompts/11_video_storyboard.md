# Step 11 — Video Storyboard Only

Chưa generate video.

## Mục tiêu
Xây storyboard 16–20 giây cho phim hero:
`FROM METHANE SIGNAL TO TRACEABLE EVIDENCE`

## Chia thành 6 shot
1. Approach
2. Measurement
3. Spatial Mapping
4. Baseline → Confirmation
5. Traceability
6. Loop Resolution

## Với mỗi shot, hãy ghi
- duration
- camera
- UAV action
- foreground/background action
- overlay state
- transition-in
- transition-out
- first-frame anchor
- last-frame anchor
- continuity risk

## Quy tắc
Transitions chỉ dùng:
motion continuation / tilt / match-position / terrain alignment / foreground occlusion / pull-back.

Không glitch, flash, whip-pan, spin, random dissolve.
