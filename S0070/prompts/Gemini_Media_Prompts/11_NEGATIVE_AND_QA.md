# Negative prompt and QA checklist

## Negative prompt

```text
Exclude weapons, missiles, soldiers, uniforms, combat, explosions, security incidents, crosshairs, circular targeting HUDs, red alert screens, command centers, real military bases, border fences, readable coordinates, maps of real restricted places, national insignia, brand logos, embedded text, fake letters, watermarks, cyberpunk neon, fantasy drones, impossible propellers, malformed landing gear, duplicated rotors, distorted hands and illegible screens.
```

## Review every output

- UAV geometry and rotors are physically plausible.
- Sensor payload looks like survey equipment, not a weapon.
- No real location, coordinate or sensitive facility is visible.
- No crosshair or targeting metaphor appears.
- No fake text is baked into an image intended to contain live UI copy.
- Anomaly color is amber and restrained, not an emergency-red alarm.
- Teal, petroleum and mist palette remains consistent.
- Image leaves the required negative space for responsive copy.
- Humans appear to review results rather than receive automated decisions.
- Media remains useful after cropping on desktop and mobile.
- Export still images as AVIF or WebP plus a high-quality source copy.
- Compress hero video and provide a separate poster image.
