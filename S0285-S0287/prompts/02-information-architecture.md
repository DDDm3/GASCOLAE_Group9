Design the page around the user's questions:
1. What is this service?
2. Why is conventional/limited measurement insufficient?
3. What exactly is measured?
4. How does measurement become biomass/carbon information?
5. What does the spatial map look like?
6. How does the service work?
7. What service level fits the project?
8. What do I receive?
9. What does this support — and what does it not replace?
10. How do I talk to a specialist?

- purpose
